I am a development economist and assistant professor at American University in Cairo, Public Policy and Administration Department, School of Global Affairs and Public Policy. Previously, I was a Research Fellow at the International Food Policy Research Institute. I received my PhD in Agricultural and Resource Economics from Berkeley in 2015, followed by a postdoctoral position at Paris School of Economics. I received a B.A. in Economics with high honors at Swarthmore College. My research agenda focuses on social protection, agriculture, and nutrition in the MENA region, in particular Yemen and Egypt, with a strong history of working closely with local partners to manage the complexities of research in fragile and politically sensitive contexts.
